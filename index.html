Robotics in 10 weeks: one school day, five subjects

Every weekday has five periods (Electronics, Maths, Mechanical/CAD, Software/ROS 2 and a Lab), so all five subjects move forward together. On Saturday the week's subjects come together in one build. The maths is applied: what a formula means, then computing and checking it in Python.

The school day

About 5.5 hours, Monday to Friday. Saturday is the workshop; Sunday is off.

10 weeks × 6 days × about 5.5 h comes to roughly 330 hours. The time saved against the six-month plan comes from running the subjects in parallel, and from simulating anything whose hardware has not arrived yet.

Rules that make the parallel plan work

Each subject feeds the labIn week 5, the line sensors from Electronics, the PD control from Maths and the printed mount from CAD all go into Saturday's line follower.
One repo per weekly buildA photo or video, a wiring diagram, measured numbers, and a section titled "What broke and how I fixed it".
Maths = Stream A, Software = Stream BThe maths period goes with lessons/day-N/; the software period goes with lessons/day-N/ros/.
Fall behind? Drop a period, not a dayMove the unfinished period to Saturday morning, and keep the five subjects in step with each other.
Stuck for 30 minutes?Write down the symptom, what you expected and what you measured, then ask. That note goes in the README too.
