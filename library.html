After six months, honestly

  • Not senior — that is 5+ years of deploying real-time systems on physical robots.
  • You will be someone who can build, debug and ship working robots across the stack, with a portfolio to prove it.
  • Entry points with no degree: robotics technician (BLS median ≈ $73.9k), teleoperation / data collection (≈ $22–35/h).
  • Robot hardware is ~25% of a robot-cell project cost; the other 75% is engineering, safety and integration.
  • Build the projects. Write down what broke. Start before you feel ready.

Prices and figures are as quoted by the source article (checked Sept 2026); verify before buying.
